This Privacy Policy explains how FantaPilot handles information when you use the app.

1. Summary

FantaPilot is designed to work primarily locally on your device. The app does not require account registration, does not ask for a user profile, and does not intentionally send your fantasy-football data to the publisher's servers.

Most of the information you create or import in the app is stored only on your device.

2. Data processed by the app

Depending on how you use FantaPilot, the app may process the following data locally on your device:

  • imported player lists and bundled player datasets
  • auction sessions, participants, rosters, budgets, and strategies
  • matchday lineup data and related scores
  • valuation settings, qualitative assignments, and saved profiles
  • assistant preferences, local model configuration, and optional downloaded local AI model files
  • exported files you choose to save from the app

3. Data storage

FantaPilot stores app data locally on the device using app-local storage and user-selected local files.

The publisher does not provide a cloud account, remote sync service, or server database for user content in the current version of the app.

4. Network connections

FantaPilot may use the network only for limited feature-specific purposes such as:

  • downloading an updated public player list from a Google Sheets export URL
  • downloading an optional local AI model from an allowed HTTPS source
  • opening the app's Google Play Store page

5. Data sent off device

FantaPilot does not intentionally transmit your locally managed fantasy data such as auctions, rosters, valuation profiles, or lineups to the publisher.

When you use network features, third-party services involved in the connection may receive technical request data that is normally exchanged over the internet, such as IP address, user agent, timestamps, and network routing information. Those third-party services operate under their own privacy policies.

6. Third-party services

  • Google Play Store
  • Google-hosted public spreadsheet export endpoints
  • Hugging Face or another explicitly allowed model download host configured in the app

7. Permissions

The Android app currently uses the INTERNET permission to support optional network features such as public dataset download, optional model download, and opening store content.

The app does not currently require account, contacts, location, SMS, call log, camera, microphone, or advertising identifier permissions for its core functionality.

8. Security

The app is designed to keep user-managed content local to the device unless you explicitly trigger a network-based feature. Network model downloads are limited to approved HTTPS hosts in the current implementation.

9. Children's privacy

FantaPilot is not specifically directed to children under the age defined by applicable law, and the publisher does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app's current functionality.

10. Your choices

  • use most app features without creating an account
  • avoid optional downloads and online updates
  • delete app-local data by using in-app reset/removal features where available
  • uninstall the app to remove locally stored app data from your device, subject to platform behavior and any exported files you saved manually

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future to reflect app changes, compliance requirements, or new features. The updated version should include a new effective date.
